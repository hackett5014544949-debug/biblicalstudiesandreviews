The Greek New Testament Project: Printing Update

November 8, 2026

I wanted to give you a quick update on the hardbound Greek New Testament. If you reserved a copy, thank you! I know you’ve been waiting, and I appreciate your patience as we work through the printing process.

Where things stand

The interior proofs were approved in early October, and the cover proof was approved in mid-October. The printer has confirmed both approvals, so that part is done.

When I heard from the printer last week, everything still looked good. The die for stamping the cover has been made, cover stamping should begin soon, and printing the text is expected to begin the week of November 9.

When will your copy ship?

Once the books are finished, they have to make their way to me. Then I’ll be packing and mailing the orders myself.

Lord willing, I expect to start shipping preorders in early to mid-December. That is an estimate, not a promised date. There are still a few steps between the printer and your mailbox, and I’ll post another update here when I have firmer news.

A quick note about addresses

If you received an email from me asking you to confirm your shipping address and haven’t replied yet, please reply to that email. Having those details settled now will help me get the books out when they arrive.

You can read it now

In the meantime, the free public-domain PDF is available. You can also use the online Greek NT reader, which includes the apparatus, tap-to-parse words, and a tool for comparing editions.

Thank you again for supporting this project. I’m looking forward to getting these books into your hands!

Stephen
