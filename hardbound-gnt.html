Hardbound printing update — September 7, 2026

An independent project of Biblical Studies and Reviews.

Thank you — the first 150 preorders sold out within the first 24 hours. New preorders are currently paused while I confirm the final order count and move the first printing into production.

The planned first printing is 500 hardbound copies of The Greek New Testament According to the Byzantine Text: 752 pages, 6″ × 9″, Smyth-sewn binding, white 40 lb. Sustana Offset Opaque paper, Sierra Midnight 5601 cloth, and foil stamping on the front and spine.

The current working estimate is approximately 16 weeks from the printer’s production start through packaging and shipment. I’ll post further updates here as production milestones are confirmed. If the print run does not proceed, presale orders will be refunded in full.

This hardbound printing is an independent project of Biblical Studies and Reviews. It is not affiliated with, published by, or endorsed by Guardian Press, LLC. The underlying public-domain edition was prepared by the Guardian Press Editorial Committee.
