The Open Byzantine Interlinear Beta

Work in progress. Not ready for publication.This reader is an unfinished draft that I am opening up for feedback. The Greek, glosses, parsing and notes may contain errors and will change. Please do not cite or redistribute it as a finished work. Found a problem? Tap any word and use “Report a problem with this word,” or send general feedback.
This is a test version. Tap any word to see its Strong's number, the full Dodson entry, how the word is parsed, and where its English gloss came from. The glosses were made by rules and an AI, then checked in part, so expect errors.

Grey gloss: Dodson's, found in the ASV verse. Green: follows the ASV Byzantine Text. Red: not found in the ASV verse, or the AI's own judgment.

Greek: Robinson-Pierpont Byzantine Textform 2018 with the six main-text changes of the 2026 Guardian Press edition (public domain).
