Digging Deep: Notetaker Deluxe

Use it without the internet

Digging Deep is a normal web page with no account and no server behind it — every note you write is saved right on your own device. That means it's already built to keep working with no internet connection, as long as you set it up the right way first. Here's the reliable way to do that.

Recommended

Install it like an app

Digging Deep can install itself onto your phone or computer like a real app, with its own icon, and it will keep working even with airplane mode on. Do this once, with a normal internet connection:

  1. Open the app below and let it fully load.
  2. On iPhone/iPad (Safari): tap the Share icon, then "Add to Home Screen."
  3. On Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
  4. On a computer (Chrome/Edge): click the install icon at the right side of the address bar, or the ⋮ menu → "Install Digging Deep…"

After that, open it from its own icon — not from this website — and it will load and save your notes with no connection at all.

Open Digging Deep →
The first time you use a particular Bible translation, dictionary, or Strong's lookup, it needs one internet connection to fetch that piece — after that, it's cached on your device and keeps working offline. Your own notes always save locally, online or off, from the very first time you open it.

Prefer a plain downloaded file?

You can also save the app itself as a single file on your computer:

  1. Open the app (button above).
  2. Press Ctrl+S (Windows) or Cmd+S (Mac) and save it as a webpage.
  3. Double-click that saved file any time to open it, no internet required, for your notes and anything you'd already loaded.

This gives you a real local copy independent of this website, but the "Install it like an app" method above is easier to keep updated and is what we'd recommend for daily use.
