Read · Watch · Discuss · Reflect

Move beyond “the Greek translation.”

Join Stephen Hackett for a guided companion course to Gregory R. Lanier and William A. Ross’s The Septuagint: What It Is and Why It Matters. Work through the book with lectures, discussion questions, and short quizzes, then bring your questions to the founding cohort’s live calls.

What you’ll work toward

  • Explain what “the Septuagint” refers to and why the term needs care.
  • Describe the origins, translation, and transmission of the Greek Old Testament.
  • Compare Greek and Hebrew readings without jumping straight from a difference to a conclusion.
  • Evaluate how the Septuagint informs Old and New Testament interpretation.

Is this for you?

For readers who want to understand why the Septuagint matters, follow the evidence, and discuss the questions it raises.

This is a guided reading and discussion course, rather than instruction in Greek grammar. Expect to read the assigned chapter before working through its lesson.

Lectures, readings, discussion questions, and quizzes form the study sequence. The launch schedule and final lesson count will be confirmed before enrollment opens.
