What this site is

A quiet desk for careful work in Scripture.

This is the home base for Stephen Hackett’s channel, Biblical Studies and Reviews. Here you’ll find video Bible study, textual criticism, and work in the original languages; a running blog for things that don’t fit neatly in a video; a Resources shelf of public-domain texts; and a path to Digging Deep: Notetaker Deluxe, the Bible study notebook app that grew out of this same habit of reading slowly.

The aim is simple: digging into the text, the manuscripts, and the questions worth resolving — without rushing past the hard places.

An independent project

The hardbound printing of that Greek New Testament is an independent project of Biblical Studies and Reviews (Stephen). It is not affiliated with, published by, or endorsed by Guardian Press. When more copies become available, the hardbound waitlist is the place to hear about it.

How the site is built

Light notes — nothing operational.

The site is hand-built static HTML — no CMS. It’s hosted on GitHub Pages from a public repository, with the domain excavatingthetext.com.

That’s enough of the plumbing for most visitors. The interesting part is still the text.

Visual identity

A field notebook feel, not a generic ministry page.

Warm manuscript palette: parchment ground, dark ink, brass and rubric accents. Display headings in a serif face; kickers and labels in a small-caps sans. Navy is reserved for quote and callout treatments — a quiet shelf-mark rather than a brand shout.
